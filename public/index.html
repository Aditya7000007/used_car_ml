02 / SCREENING
⌁

Age + mileage rule

The flag is set when age is at least 8 years and odometer reading is at least 70,000 km. It is a project heuristic, not a real-world risk model.

03 / SIMILARITY
◎

Comparable listings

K-means assigns a broad market segment. Nearest Neighbors finds listings with similar age, usage, economy, engine, power, and seating.
